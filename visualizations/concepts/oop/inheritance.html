Key Concepts:
Inheritance: Dog inherits eat() and breathe() from Animal — it doesn't need to redefine them.
Override: Dog has its OWN makeSound() — it replaces Animal's version entirely.
Extension: Dog adds fetch() which Animal doesn't have.
Multi-level: Puppy extends Dog, inheriting everything from Dog (and therefore Animal too).
Polymorphism: When you call makeSound() on any Animal reference, each subclass runs its OWN version.
