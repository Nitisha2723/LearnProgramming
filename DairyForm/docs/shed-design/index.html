FULLY AUTOMATIC

🥛 Milking System

  • 2x cluster milking machines (auto pulsation)
  • CIP auto-cleaning after every milking session
  • Milk pipeline direct to bulk cooler
  • Auto milk fat/SNF test after collection
  • Temperature logged every 15 minutes
  • Alert on phone if cooler temp exceeds 6°C
FULLY AUTOMATIC

🌾 Feeding System

  • TMR mixer prepares ration at 5 AM & 3 PM
  • Conveyor belt delivers feed along manger
  • Chaff cutter auto-triggered before mixing
  • Weigh scale logs quantity each batch
  • Alert if feed not consumed (animal health)
  • Concentrate & mineral auto-dispensed
FULLY AUTOMATIC

💧 Water System

  • Solar pump fills overhead tanks automatically
  • Float valve drinkers — always full, never overflow
  • Water level sensor alerts on low tank
  • Drip irrigation to fodder fields on timer
  • Wash bay on timer (twice daily in summer)
  • All water recycled — wash water to biogas
FULLY AUTOMATIC

🧹 Manure Management

  • Floor scraper runs at 5 AM and 6 PM on timer
  • Scrapes dung to collection pit automatically
  • Dung flows by gravity to biogas plant inlet
  • Biogas slurry flows to compost yard
  • No manual cleaning of shed floor needed
  • Gas production monitored by pressure gauge
FULLY AUTOMATIC

🌡 Climate Control

  • Temperature sensor at 1.5m height in shed
  • Exhaust fans auto-ON at 30°C, OFF at 27°C
  • Mist fogger auto-ON at 32°C in shed
  • Exercise yard sprinklers at 33°C (summer)
  • Humidity sensor — prevents respiratory disease
  • Phone alert if temp exceeds 35°C
FULLY AUTOMATIC

🔌 Power System

  • 5 kW solar panels on shed roof
  • Lithium battery — 4 hrs backup at night
  • Grid-tied — sell surplus power to APSPDCL
  • AMF panel auto-starts genset on power cut
  • Biogas connected to genset as fuel backup
  • Power consumption app on mobile
FULLY AUTOMATIC

📷 Security & Monitoring

  • 8 CCTV cameras — shed, parlour, entry, store
  • Motion detection alert on phone
  • IoT ear tags — activity, temperature, estrus
  • Estrus (heat) detection alert — best AI time
  • Farm management app — all data in one place
  • CCTV accessible remotely 24/7
SEMI-AUTOMATIC

🚿 Hygiene & Bio-security

  • Entry gate mist arch — auto spray on entry
  • Foot bath at gate and milking parlour entry
  • Disinfectant changed manually every 3 days
  • Pressure wash of shed by worker — 30 min/day
  • Animal wash bay — timer-triggered twice daily
  • Tick/parasite spray — weekly manual
SEMI-AUTOMATIC

⚕️ Animal Health

  • IoT ear tag fever alert — auto notification
  • Platform weigh scale — weekly weight check
  • Vaccination schedule in farm app (reminder)
  • Vet call — manual (but app tracks history)
  • Calf auto milk feeder — programmed volumes
  • Pregnancy & calving records in farm app
SEMI-AUTOMATIC

🌿 Fodder Production

  • Drip irrigation on timer — fully automatic
  • Fertilizer injection through drip (fertigation)
  • Harvesting — manual (1 worker, 2 hrs/week)
  • Chaff cutting — machine-assisted (30 min/day)
  • Silage making — seasonal, manual + machine
  • Napier regrows — no replanting for 5 years
