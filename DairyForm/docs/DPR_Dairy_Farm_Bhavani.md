## 3. PROJECT DESCRIPTION

### 3.1 Project Concept

Smt. Danthuluri Bhavani proposes to establish a **40-head fully automated buffalo dairy farm** on 3.5 acres of leased agricultural land at Devunipalavalasa Village, Srikakulam District, Andhra Pradesh. The farm will be set up under the **NABARD Dairy Entrepreneurship Development Scheme (DEDS)** and will adopt modern automation technologies to enable operation by 1–2 workers.

The project aims to:
- Produce approximately 350–400 litres of high-quality buffalo milk daily
- Supply milk to Vijaya Dairy / APDDCF / private dairies at remunerative prices
- Generate sustainable livelihood and rural employment
- Adopt zero-waste practices through biogas and organic manure

### 3.2 Project Rationale

- Srikakulam District has strong demand for milk with organized procurement through Vijaya Dairy
- Buffalo milk commands premium price (₹50–58/litre) due to higher fat content
- Murrah buffalo breed is ideally suited to AP's agro-climatic conditions
- Government of India and AP State Government actively support dairy through subsidies
- Growing demand for A2 buffalo milk in urban markets
- Applicant owns 3.5 acres suitable for fodder cultivation, reducing feed costs by 40%

## 12. SWOT ANALYSIS

### Strengths
- Women entrepreneur — maximum subsidy (33.33%)
- Own land (leased from father) — no land purchase cost
- Automated farm — low labor dependency
- AP has strong milk procurement infrastructure (Vijaya Dairy)
- Buffalo milk commands premium price

### Weaknesses
- First-time dairy entrepreneur (mitigation: hire experienced farm manager initially)
- Dependence on purchased concentrate feed (mitigation: expand fodder area)

### Opportunities
- Growing demand for A2 buffalo milk in urban/export markets
- Scope to add value-added products (ghee, paneer) in Phase 2
- Carbon credits from biogas plant
- AP government's push for dairy sector growth

### Threats
- Milk price fluctuation (mitigation: tie-up with Vijaya Dairy at fixed rates)
- Disease outbreak (mitigation: insurance + vaccination protocol)
- Fodder shortage in drought years (mitigation: silage storage)
